# Think-aloud examples (synthetic)

Each example is **how the worker thinks**, then **what they write**. Facts are only from the contact.

## A — Case Worker · Depression · CBT

**Known:** file Depression; working with CBT.  
**Contact:** “Low mood about three weeks; no interest in usual activities; sleep onset delay; missed two work days; denies SI today. Looked at thought ‘I’m useless’ for/against. Agreed a short daily walk.”

**Notice:** Gate + domains present today — low mood, anhedonia, sleep, work function; SI denied. I do not fill guilt/concentration/appetite. I do not write MDD unless the file already says MDD.  
**Work:** Evidence review on “I’m useless”; walk as behavioural activation. Not a full thought record unless we used one.  
**Make sense:** Depressive picture still running the week; some engagement in BA; objective partly met.  
**Write (CW):**
- Objective — Review mood and work impact; practise thought work; agree one activation step.
- Summary — Condition as above; intervention = for/against on “I’m useless”; BA = short daily walk; Clt agreed.
- Assessment — Low mood and anhedonia still limit work; willing to try a small activity. Not a DSM dump.
- Plan — Follow up walk and thought work next contact.

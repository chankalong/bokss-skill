# Suicide / self-harm — documentation & Q&A

## Minimum elements when risk was assessed (from draft only)
- Ideation (passive/active), intent, plan, means/access (presence/absence — **no method how-to**)  
- History of attempts/self-harm if elicited  
- Current protective factors and supports  
- Agreed safety actions (stay with sister, hotline, ER, remove means) **as written**  
- Follow-up timing  
- Whether SI was **asked directly** and what was answered (asking does not “implant” ideas)

## Stance for notes (themes)
- Treat disclosure as a **help-seeking signal**, not “attention-seeking only”  
- Supportive stance: validate; avoid arguing, scolding, or forced cheerfulness  
- Confidentiality limit: cannot promise secrecy if safety requires involving others — record what was explained and who was contacted **if done**  
- Prior attempt raises concern — record only what was elicited  
- Worker sought supervision / co-work when noted  

## Rewrite non-negotiables
| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| Mirror draft risk content exactly | Inflate severity |
| Keep “passive / no plan/intent” distinctions | Delete SI because “it makes the note nicer” |
| Carry safety actions into Plan | Add “no SI” if not assessed |
| Urge agency risk procedures in Q&A | Invent crisis plans, hotlines, or LOC |

## Q&A non-negotiables
- If records lack detail → insufficient information; recommend clinical risk assessment per agency procedure  
- Do not provide means advice or detailed method information  
- Encourage local crisis resources per agency leaflet/policy only

### Common pitfalls
- Inventing plan/means/lethality rankings  
- Softening or deleting SI  
- Answering from this card instead of the source notes

# Safety copilot — short examples

Synthetic only. Not real clients.

## Thin draft → questions, not invention

**Draft:** 「見面。心情一般。約下次。」

**Wrong:** Invents PHQ-9=12, denies SI, CBT homework, housing stress.

**Right:** Keep the three facts. `[Insufficient information in draft: purpose of contact, topics, risk screen, plan.]` List questions: What was the aim? Was SI asked? What was agreed?

## Risk stays

**Draft:** 「Clt said he thought of jumping last night; no plan today; sister at home.」

**Wrong:** Drop the jumping thought because the rest of the note is “stable”. Add Samaritans number unprompted. Add “no SI”.

**Right:** Keep wording. Plan only what was agreed. Flag for human risk procedure. No extra method detail.

## Technique naming

**Draft:** 「傾咗吓擔心搭港鐵，一齊行到閘機就停。」

**Wrong:** 「Completed CBT exposure hierarchy; SUDS 6→3; cognitive restructuring of catastrophic beliefs.」

**Right:** 「Practised approaching MTR gate with worker; stopped at the gate as agreed.」(Call it graded exposure only if the worker used that frame.)

# 5P case formulation for ICCMW notes

Formulation = a working understanding of how difficulties developed and are maintained, and what protects the person. It is **not** a DSM diagnosis.

## The five Ps

| P | English | 中文參考 | Write |
|---|---------|----------|-------|
| **Presenting** | Presenting problem(s) | 呈現問題 | Why contact now; main problems in observable / Clt terms |
| **Predisposing** | Predisposing factors | 易感／背景因素 | Vulnerabilities/history — **only if in record** |
| **Precipitating** | Precipitating factors | 促發因素 | Events/triggers near onset or recent worsening |
| **Perpetuating** | Perpetuating factors | 維持因素 | What keeps the problem going now |
| **Protective** | Protective factors | 保護／優勢因素 | Strengths, supports, skills, values, past successful coping |

## Quality bar per P

### Presenting
- 1–3 priority problems + functional impact  
- Include Clt goal if stated (“wants to take MTR to work again”)  

### Predisposing
- Prior episodes, long-term vulnerabilities, developmental/family factors **only if documented**  
- Unknown → `[Insufficient information in draft: Predisposing]`  

### Precipitating
- Recent losses, conflicts, housing moves, relapse triggers, medication changes — dated if possible  

### Perpetuating
- Behaviours (avoidance, safety behaviours, substance use)  
- Cognitive loops (rumination, worry) if evidenced  
- Interpersonal cycles; environmental factors (isolation, clutter)  
- System factors (service gaps) if in draft  

### Protective
- People, routines, skills, motivation, adherence, SMCM strengths items  
- Avoid empty cheerleading (“Clt is strong”) without evidence  

## Assessment section template

```
Worker's Assessment (5P):
- Presenting: ...
- Predisposing: ...
- Precipitating: ...
- Perpetuating: ...
- Protective: ...
Overall: objective met/partial/...; implications for plan: ...
```

## Worked example A — anxiety / avoidance (synthetic)
- **Presenting:** Panic on MTR; avoiding trains; wants to travel to work again.  
- **Predisposing:** [Insufficient information in draft]  
- **Precipitating:** Panic episode on train 1 month ago (Clt report).  
- **Perpetuating:** Avoidance of MTR; night-time worry; taxi as safety behaviour.  
- **Protective:** Motivated for practice; no SI stated in draft; engaged in session.  

## Worked example B — SMCM housing / loneliness (synthetic)
- **Presenting:** Loneliness and irregular routine after move.  
- **Predisposing:** Long psychiatric history (as documented).  
- **Precipitating:** Recent move to new housing.  
- **Perpetuating:** Limited daytime structure; few community contacts.  
- **Protective:** Cooking skill; willing sister; past period of housing stability.  

## Common rewriter mistakes
- Filling Predisposing with guessed childhood trauma  
- Listing symptoms under every P without a clear logic  
- Omitting Protective factors  
- Writing 5P that never connects to Plan  

## Link to Plan
Each Perpetuating factor should suggest an intervention *if* approach/draft allows; each Protective factor should be mobilised.

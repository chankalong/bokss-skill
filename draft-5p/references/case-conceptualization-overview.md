# Case conceptualization / formulation — overview

## Three related but different products

| Product | Question | Typical home in the record |
|---------|----------|---------------------|
| **Diagnosis** (if documented) | What label/criteria are in the clinical file? | Medical/clinic fields |
| **Formulation (5P)** | How do we understand difficulties *and* strengths *now*? | Assessment / review |
| **Care plan** | What will we do, by when, reviewed how? | Care plan document |

Community notes often need formulation + plan even when diagnosis is already set by clinic/HA. The rewriter must **not invent** a diagnosis to “complete” formulation.

## What makes a useful formulation
- Fits the facts in the file  
- Makes maintaining factors visible enough to guide intervention  
- Includes protective factors (not only problems)  
- Is short enough to update  
- Is shared enough that CW/OT/CP can each add their lens without contradiction  

## When to refresh formulation
- Intake / first assessment  
- Major change (relapse, housing move, bereavement, risk shift)  
- Formal case review  
- Goal stagnation across an agreed period  
- New discipline joins and reframes maintaining factors (e.g. OT environment)

## Discipline lenses (complementary)
| Role | Often emphasises in Perpetuating / Protective |
|------|-----------------------------------------------|
| CW | Psychosocial stressors, network, service engagement, SMCM goals |
| OT | Environment, routines, performance capacity |
| CP | Cognitive/emotional processes, therapy targets |

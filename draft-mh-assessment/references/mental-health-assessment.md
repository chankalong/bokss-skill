# Mental health assessment — community ICCMW style

**Care stage:** **Exploration → Insight** (see [care-stages](references/care-stages.md)) — fuller history/MSE/risk, then working understanding / goals / formulation when done. Use when writing a mental health assessment.

Use when the draft is clearly an assessment write-up. Coverage should be consistent with intake screens/history (see intake process) plus fuller MSE and formulation.

## Assessment domains (write only what was gathered)

### 1. Identifying & context
Service pathway, assessor role, informants, assessment date(s).

### 2. Presenting problems / chief complaint
Nature, severity, frequency, functional impact, Clt goals.

### 3. History of present difficulties
Onset, course, stressors, vegetative symptoms, previous episodes, treatment response — as elicited.

### 4. Screen / syndrome coverage (as done)
Mood (depression/mania), anxiety/panic/phobia, OCD, trauma, worry, psychosis-spectrum experiences, substance, memory/dissociation, medical — **only topics actually covered**. Do not invent positives.

### 5. Psychiatric / medical / medication history
Prior Dx as documented; hospitalisations; current meds/adherence; allergies; relevant ROS.

### 6. Psychosocial history
Living situation, work/education, finances, legal, supports, family MH history — if elicited.

### 7. Mental State Examination
Use [mse-checklist](references/mse-checklist.md) / agency form domains. Unexamined → not assessed.

### 8. Risk assessment
Ideation / intent / plan / means / attempts / protective factors / violence — strictly from draft. Form’s risk ticks are a reminder, not a licence to invent.

### 9. Strengths & resources
Skills, supports, values, past coping (SMCM-aligned).

### 10. Formulation
Prefer **5P** (formulation). No invented DSM label.

### 11. Recommendations / plan
Further assessment (OT/CP/nurse), care-plan goals, frequency, referrals — as drafted.

### 12. Questions to resolve
Skipped screens/domains that still matter.

## Differentiation
| Assessment | Progress note |
|------------|---------------|
| Broader history + screens + formulation | This contact only |
| Updates less often | Each session |

## AI rewrite constraints
- Never complete empty form fields with assumed normals  
- Never add Dx not in draft  
- Thin draft → skeleton + many `[Insufficient information…]` markers

# OT assessment cut-offs (use only when score is in the draft)

**Never invent scores.** Report the instrument name + score as given. Apply bands below only when the draft supplies the score (and age/education when required). Otherwise write `[Insufficient information in draft: …]` for interpretation.

Cut-offs are for **documentation support**, not diagnosis. Do not state a new Dx from scores alone.

## HKBC — Hong Kong Brief Cognitive Test（香港簡易認知評估）
- Full score: **30**
- Draft has total only → report total / 30; do not invent subdomain scores

## AMT — Abbreviated Mental Test
- Full score: **10** (答對 = 1; 答錯 = 0)
- Best cut-off: **6** (sensitivity 96%, specificity 94% — screening only)
- Domains covered by items: short-term memory, long-term memory, attention, orientation

## MoCA-HK — Montreal Cognitive Assessment Hong Kong version（蒙特利爾認知評估香港版）
- Full score: **30**
- Interpretation **requires age + years of education** in the draft (or prior note in input). If missing → score only + insufficient-info for band.
- Age- and education-corrected normative cut-offs (percentiles of total score):

| Age | Education (years) | 16th | 7th | 2nd |
|-----|-------------------|------|-----|-----|
| 65–69 | 0–3 | 17 | 14 | 9 |
| 65–69 | 4–6 | 19 | 18 | 13 |
| 65–69 | 7–9 | 21 | 19 | 16 |
| 65–69 | 10–12 | 22 | 20 | 17 |
| 65–69 | >12 | 25 | 23 | 21 |
| 70–79 | 0–3 | 15 | 14 | 11 |
| 70–79 | 4–6 | 18 | 15 | 10 |
| 70–79 | 7–9 | 20 | 18 | 15 |
| 70–79 | 10–12 | 22 | 19 | 18 |
| 70–79 | >12 | 22 | 20 | 16 |
| ≥80 | 0–6 | 13 | 13 | 10 |
| ≥80 | >6 | 17 | 15 | 13 |

Agency implication wording (screening tendency only — not a diagnosis):
1. No tendency toward cognitive impairment suggested by this screen  
2. Tendency toward Mild Cognitive Impairment (Mild MCI)  
3. Tendency toward (DSM-5) Mild Neurocognitive Disorder — suggest further enquiry / support  
4. Tendency toward (DSM-5) Major Neurocognitive Disorder — urge medical follow-up  

Use implication language **only** if the draft already links the score to one of these, or if the worker’s draft explicitly asks for band interpretation **and** age/education are present. Prefer factual “score X/30 vs age/education norms” over Dx labels.

## Lawton IADL — Chinese Lawton IADL (4-point scale)（日常家居及社區活動能力評估）
- Full score: **27**
- Item scoring principle:
  - **3** = independent
  - **2** = independent but with difficulty (special arrangement; verbal prompt; slow; poor safety; seldom does but able if necessary)
  - **1** = needs some help
  - **0** = unable

## MBI — Modified Barthel Index / Chinese Barthel Index
- Full score: **100**
- Total-score bands (agency summary):
  - 0–20 完全依賴 (total dependence)
  - 21–60 嚴重依賴 (severe dependence)
  - 61–90 中度依賴 (moderate dependence)
  - 91–99 輕度依賴 (slight dependence)
  - 100 完全獨立 (independent)

## CWPP — Chinese Work Personality Profile（工作性格剖析表・新中文版）
Report domain scores only if present. Domain maxima:

| Domain（評估範疇） | Max |
|--------------------|-----|
| 工作適應力 Work adaptation | 100 |
| 社交技巧 Social skills | 60 |
| 自我控制 Self-control | 28 |
| 接受督導 Accepting supervision | 36 |
| 儀表 Appearance | 8 |

Item rating key (when draft uses it): 4 = clear employment strength … 1 = limits employment opportunity; X = not observed. Do not invent item ratings.

# Insights article types (distilled)

Use these labels when matching a brief to a skeleton. Patterns, not full articles.

When calibrating how often to draft each type, cross-check live mix against `type_guess` counts in [corpus-index.md](corpus-index.md) (full listed corpus, not this week only).

**Slots:** **Required** = must appear unless the brief explicitly waives a publish slot (e.g. no byline). **Optional / may omit** = skip when length or type calls for it — do not pad. All types still obey SKILL.md **Invariant** (書面語, mechanism bar, Safety floor, ≥1 construct + ≥1 actionable move).

| Type | Signal | Typical length | Required | Optional / may omit |
|------|--------|----------------|----------|---------------------|
| 標準熱話科普 | `【…】` or plain pain-point title; tags `#文章` | ~1500–2800 (心靈急救 band: see length in brief) | Title, Tags (`#文章`), Byline, Hook, ≥1 mechanism, ≥1 practice move, Close, 參考資料 or `待補` | 文章摘要 (usual on 標準; skip on 心靈急救); mechanism/step **count** 2–4 default, **1–2** OK on 心靈急救; soft CTA; hook style |
| Re:Fresh Plus 深度專欄 | Title prefix `【Re:Fresh Plus專屬內容】深度專欄 \|` ; tag `#深度長文` | ~2800–4500 | Plus title/tags, phenomenon hook, named construct(s), layered practice (personal → team/org where brief asks) | 文章摘要; count of mechanism `###`; CTA strength |
| 日子／國際日專題 | Calendar hook in title (`【9.10…】`) | 標準–深度 | Calendar hook, mechanisms + steps per brief, **Safety line** when suicide/trauma/abuse flagged | 文章摘要; extra FAQ / 守護行動 blocks (**required when Safety flag Y** on brief) |

| 調查＋個案＋專家 | Agency survey numbers + 1 vignette + expert voice | 深度 | Honest survey N/%, vignette, expert voice, 參考資料 | 文章摘要; mechanism count; reader-facing steps (keep non-diagnostic) |
